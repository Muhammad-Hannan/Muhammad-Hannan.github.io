A little about my work

Water systems are complex. Better data can help us see them clearly.

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Hydrology and Water Resources at Hohai University in Nanjing, China. My work brings together satellite gravimetry, remote sensing, and machine learning to better understand water storage and its change.

I’m especially interested in translating large-scale observations into meaningful insight for groundwater monitoring and water security in data-sparse regions.
